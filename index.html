What is LooksRare?

LooksRare is a community-first NFT marketplace for buying, selling, making offers on, and browsing digital collectibles. The official LooksRare Docs cover marketplace guides, developer resources, token information, rewards, and protocol contracts.

How LooksRare works

  1. Open the appGo to the official domain and confirm looksrare.org before connecting a wallet.
  2. Connect walletUse a supported wallet or WalletConnect, then review each signature request before approving.
  3. Find a collectionSearch collections, compare listings, offers, activity, and item details inside the marketplace.
  4. Trade or manageBuy listed NFTs, make offers, list owned NFTs, or open Rewards from the same app.

Before you trade

Marketplace routing

The app brings collection discovery, buying, selling, offers, raffles, and rewards into one official destination.

The aggregator documentation describes buying ERC721 and ERC1155 tokens from multiple marketplaces in a single transaction.

LooksRare FAQ

What is looksrare used for?

looksrare is used to explore NFT collections, buy listed NFTs, make offers, sell owned NFTs, and access marketplace-related rewards through the official app.

Is looksrare the official app?

The official app is hosted at looksrare.org; verify the domain carefully before connecting a wallet or signing any transaction.

What wallet do I need for looksrare?

LooksRare supports wallet connections through WalletConnect and compatible crypto wallets; always review signatures and network prompts before approving.

Can I buy and sell NFTs on looksrare?

Yes. The official marketplace includes guides for buying fixed-price NFTs, making offers, listing NFTs for sale, and accepting offers.

Is looksrare financial advice?

No. Marketplace pages, rankings, rewards, and token references are informational; evaluate NFT risks, contract prompts, and liquidity before acting.
